# Recipes

Each recipe is a complete, maintained example embedded from the `docs/guides` reference, followed by what the application must still own.

> [!note] Recipes are local-only
> They deliberately do not save to a server. Treat persistence, retries, and rollback as your work.

## Using a recipe well

1. Copy it into a page beneath `Root`.
2. Run it as-is first and confirm the behavior.
3. Replace the local state with your real data layer.
4. Work through its *Application responsibilities* list.

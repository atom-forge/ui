## Request

Move TreeView away from the Atlaskit drag-and-drop wrapper before consolidating the remaining DnD implementations.

## What Was Done

- Replaced the TreeView Atlaskit action usage with native HTML5 drag-and-drop events.
- Preserved the existing move contract and validation for child kinds, maximum depth, cycles, and application `canDrop` rules.

## Notes

TreeView no longer imports the shared Atlaskit-based action helper. The helper and Atlaskit dependencies remain because Calendar, TableEditor, and DropIndicator still consume them.
